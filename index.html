Why Your Brain May Not Be Performing at Its Best

After age 40, many people notice changes in their ability to recall names, stay focused, or think as quickly as they used to. Research suggests this is often linked to disrupted Gamma brainwave activity.

What are Gamma waves? Gamma brainwaves (approximately 40Hz) are the frequency associated with peak cognitive performance — sharp memory, fast thinking, and clear focus. Modern stress, poor sleep, and screen overload can significantly suppress this activity.
